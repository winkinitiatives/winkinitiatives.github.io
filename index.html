Profiles of the writers, musicians, actors, dancers, poets, and ministers advancing the Perfection agenda.
Highlights from Event X, the flagship annual experience hosted since 2017 to disciple audiences through Spirit-filled acts.

Osagyefo Ampem Foundation

Charitable arm of the Wenchi Traditional Area focused on the well-being of families across Bono Region through education access, health support, and livelihoods programs guided by Osagyefo Ampem Anye Amoampong Tabrako III.

Wheels of Change equips schoolchildren in remote communities with bicycles so daily treks no longer keep them from class.
Five-year collaboration with Trans Sahara Industries expands electric bicycles for nurses, caregivers, and vulnerable farmers.
Health outreaches, agriculture support, and youth employment initiatives lift households beyond transport access.
Community leadership keeps projects accountable to local needs while scaling impact across Ghana.
